Services

Four Areas of Focus

My consulting practice is built around four connected pillars — strategy, technology, AI and executive advisory — designed to move an organisation from diagnosis to measurable transformation.

01

Strategy & Business Transformation

Before any technology or AI decision is made, leadership needs clarity on where the organisation is today, where it needs to go, and what is preventing growth. I work with leadership teams to understand the current operating model and design what needs to change.

Business strategy Growth strategy Operating model transformation Organisational transformation Performance improvement Business model development Strategic planning
02

Digital & Technology Transformation

Technology should support business objectives, not drive them. I help organisations understand how their systems, data and processes should evolve to support the strategy — bridging business requirements with technical execution. I am a senior leader who understands technology, not a software developer for hire.

Digital transformation Technology strategy Systems transformation Process redesign Workflow optimisation Technology roadmap Data & information flows Technology investment decisions Technology/business alignment
03

AI Transformation

AI is not a strategy in itself, and it isn't the solution to every problem. My work is to identify where AI can materially improve business performance, and to integrate it into the organisation's operating model. Business problem first. Technology second. AI where it creates real value.

AI strategy AI readiness assessment AI opportunity mapping AI operating models AI-enabled workflows AI agents Business process automation AI workforce strategy AI implementation roadmap AI adoption strategy Productivity improvement Management information & decision support
04

Executive Advisory

Senior-level advisory support for CEOs, MDs, COOs, CTOs and boards navigating transformation decisions. This is access to an experienced strategic adviser — not a generic monthly consulting subscription. For the right relationship, this can evolve into a retained advisory arrangement.

AI investment decisions Technology strategy Transformation priorities Business growth Operating model decisions Technology/vendor evaluation Digital transformation AI adoption Strategic technology decisions
